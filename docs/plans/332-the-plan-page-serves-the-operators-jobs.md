## 1. Problem

The plan page answers "what is in this one plan", and the operator's questions are about the space: what waits on me, what is running, what the loop takes next, and which plans are finished. Each of those needs a visit to every plan, and some cannot be answered or acted on from the page at all.

What the page is. `SpacePlanPage` (`packages/web-product/src/ui/screens/space-plan.tsx`) reads the whole space's tree (`GET /v1/spaces/:spaceId/plan`) and shows one root at a time: a horizontal rail of tabs, one per root with its step count, the first by position selected; a **Phase** filter; an **Activity** filter (All, In flight, Idle) with two sentences explaining it; a **Done** window (Today, 7 days, All); the root's title and four tally chips (`done`, `active`, `decide`, `dropped`); five columns — Up next, In progress, Needs you, Done, Dropped — whose column is `planBoardColumn` (`waiting` → Up next, any other open non-decide node → In progress, `blocked` or an open `decide` → Needs you); and a 360px rail per card with the node's goal, criteria, outcome, runs, findings and the close controls (**Mark done**, **Drop**, **Decide**). Selection lives in component state; the read is not live.

What the page shows in the operator's space (Full Circle, read on 2026-10-27: nine roots, 106 steps):

- **What waits on the operator is spread over three tabs and says nothing about why.** Eight steps stand under Needs you — three in Plan 331, one in Plan 330, four in Plan 320 — every one `blocked`, none of kind `decide`, so the Workbench carries none of them. The default tab, Plan 324, has none. Why each waits is in its note ("Proposed from the merged plan document; awaits the operator's approval", "needs a scratch repository and a GitHub binding", "Awaits the operator's go; queued after Plan 330"), and the rail does not show the note.
- **The only thing the operator wants to do to them cannot be done here.** Approving a proposal or lifting a stop is a move from `blocked` to `active`; the page's write accepts `done` and `dropped` only (`PlanNodeCloseSchema`). Reopening a closed step and reordering steps or plans have no control either. Plan 331 D2 makes root order the resident's priority and calls reordering "a write the board already makes"; it is not.
- **Four steps under Needs you will never be taken.** Plan 320's P6.a–d are `blocked` under P6, which is `done`. The stream walk does not descend through a closed node and `listOpenDecisions` skips anything under one, so approving them would change nothing. The board shows them exactly like the others.
- **The columns do not say what the loop is doing.** No step in the space is `waiting`, so Up next is empty in every plan. In progress holds eight steps; one has a run in flight (Plan 332 P0), and the other seven are idle — approved work the loop has not taken, two of them titled "taken by the operator outside the loop". The Activity filter and its two sentences exist to correct what the In progress column says.
- **A plan's state is invisible.** Every root is `active`. Plans 322, 324 and 328 have every step closed and read exactly like Plan 331, which waits on the operator, and Plan 332, which is running. The first tab, the one the page opens on, is a finished plan.
- **The rail of tabs is wider than the page.** At 1280px the nine tabs measure 3,185px in a 1,160px row: three are visible. The row clips rather than scrolls, so the other six cannot be reached with a pointer; reaching one by keyboard focus scrolls the page's content container sideways and leaves the board off screen (observed selecting Plan 315 and Plan 332).
- **Nothing on the page can be linked to.** Reloading returns to the first tab with no step open; a Workbench item, a Helmsman reply or a help article cannot point at a plan or a step.

What breaks as plans accumulate. At **14 plans**: the tab row is about 5,000px with three visible; finding what needs the operator is fourteen tab visits; finished plans keep their positions at the front. At **50 plans**: the tab row is about 17,000px; every view of one plan reads all fifty trees with every done step (`readPlanTrees` walks the whole space, `PLAN_TREE_WALK_BOUNDS` 2,000 nodes — today 106 nodes are 91 KB with every done step, 33 KB with today's), so a few plans the size of Plan 315 (72 steps) reach the bound and later plans read short or not at all (`truncated`); and the tallies of fifty roots are computed to show one.

## 3. Decisions

**D1 — The page opens on the space, not on a plan: an overview across plans answers J1–J3, and a plan's board is one level below it.** The landing view has three lanes — **Needs you**, **In flight**, **Up next** — each a list of steps from every open plan, tagged with their plan; beside them the plan list (D2). Choosing a plan opens its board; choosing a step anywhere opens the same rail. The board stays, for J5–J7 inside one plan. _Rejected:_ the tab per plan with a Needs-you count on each tab — the operator still visits each, and a count does not say why or offer the action. _Rejected:_ one board with every plan's cards in five columns — Done alone would hold hundreds of cards, and a card without its plan's context cannot be judged.

**D2 — Plans are a vertical list in position order, each row carrying its state; the tab rail goes.** The list is the left column of the page (280px): open plans in position order — the order the resident takes them (Plan 331 D2) — each row the plan's title, its state (D3), its progress (closed of steps) and its non-zero counts of steps needing the operator and in flight. Below them **Finished · N** (open roots whose steps are all closed), each with **Close plan**, and **Closed · N**, folded. The selected row scrolls into view. Below 900px the list is a disclosure above the content. _Rejected:_ tabs with scroll arrows or an overflow menu — a one-line tab has no room for state, and an overflow menu hides exactly the plans that need looking at. _Rejected:_ grouping open plans by state — the order of open plans is the operator's priority, and grouping by state would hide it.

**D3 — A plan's state is derived by one rule on the server and stored nowhere.** For a root (in precedence order): `closed` — the root is `done` or `dropped`; `needs_you` — a step under it stands in the Needs you column (D4); `in_flight` — a step under it stands in In flight; `queued` — it has open steps, all in Up next; `finished` — it has steps and every one is closed; `empty` — it has none. A plan that both needs the operator and has a run in flight reads `needs_you` with both counts on its row. `finished` is not a status: the root stays open until the operator closes it, which is the existing close write on the root. _Rejected:_ closing a root automatically when its last step closes — Plan 322 D2 says a node is done against its own criteria, never from its children. _Rejected:_ deriving it in the browser — the overview does not ship the trees it would be derived from (D7).

**D4 — The columns name what the loop is doing with a step, read from status and activity: Needs you, In flight, Up next, Done, Dropped.** This changes Plan 322 P3.3's column rule, not the statuses. The new `planBoardColumn` reads `kind`, `status`, `activity` and whether an ancestor is closed, first match deciding: `done` → Done; `dropped` → Dropped; open under a closed ancestor → Needs you (reason `stranded`); an open `decide` node → Needs you (`decide`); `blocked` → Needs you (`blocked`); `waiting`, or `activity` `in_flight` → In flight; otherwise (`active`, idle) → Up next. So Up next is Plan 322's approved queue — idle `active` steps — listed in walk order (parents before children, siblings by position), and the step `findActiveStreamNode` would take for its root carries **Next**. A `waiting` step is the loop's, on a run, whether the run is still going or has ended and the loop has yet to advance it; its card says which. An idle `active` step whose pull request is open stays in Up next with the pull request on its card: no run of the node is on it, and the walk holds there (P2b's `wait`) until it merges. The Activity filter and its explanation go: the column now carries activity. Columns read left to right in the order they ask for attention — Needs you first. _Why the change:_ under the P3.3 rule Up next was `waiting` only, which no step in the space is, and In progress held approved work no run was on; the operator could not read the queue or the work in flight from the columns. _Rejected:_ keeping the rule and the Activity filter — the filter's two sentences are the evidence the columns mislead. _Rejected:_ a new status such as `queued` — the statuses are the loop's vocabulary (Plan 322 D2), the stream walk reads them, and the board's job is to show them, not to add to them.

**D5 — Needs you carries why and what to do.** A Needs-you row or card shows the reason (**Decision**, **Blocked**, **Under a closed step**), the note's first line (`noteHead`, at most 160 characters, as `goalHead`), and how long since the node was last written. Its action is in reach: **Decide** for a decision, **Approve** for a blocked step (D6), and for a stranded one **Reopen <parent>** or **Drop**. The rail gains **Now** — the note's first line — above Goal, and the whole note under **Working note**, folded. _Rejected:_ a Workbench item for every blocked step — asking is Plan 331 P2's, and a Workbench answer is free text the resident would interpret as a grant of scope, which Plan 331 D5 rejects; the Workbench keeps the `decide` item (Plan 322 D9) and nothing else from the plan.

**D6 — The operator approves, reopens and orders from the page, through writes of their own.** Two writes beside the close, each a person's only (`planWriteAuthority.ts`), compare-and-set on the revision the rail or row read (Plan 322 D6), stamped from the session, with an audit row of its own:

- **Open** — `PATCH /v1/spaces/:spaceId/plan/nodes/:nodeId/open`, `PlanNodeOpenSchema` `{ expectedRevision, why?, spendCents? }`, moves a `blocked`, `done` or `dropped` node to `active` through `updatePlanNode`, and the note it leaves follows the engine's rule for each. On a `blocked` node the control is **Approve**, and `why` (within the note storage ceiling) is supplied as `Approved: …` above the note as it stands, so the reason is the note's first line and **Now** reads it. On a closed node the control is **Reopen**; the engine (`reopenedNote`) writes its own `Reopened; it was <status>: <outcome>` as the first line, and a supplied `Reopened: …` follows it and replaces the prior note — the working note of the closed work — while no `why` keeps the prior note under the engine's line. So after a reopen **Now** reads the outcome being undone, and the reason is the line after it. _Rejected:_ changing `reopenedNote` to put the operator's reason first — the next wake must first read what the node claimed before it was reopened, which is why the engine keeps it there, and the open write is not the place to change that rule for every caller. _Rejected:_ keeping the prior note under the reason on a reopen — it describes work the reopen declares unfinished, and the engine's line already carries what that work claimed. An `active` or `waiting` node is `422 PLAN_NODE_UNCHANGED`. Audit action `plan.node.open`, with the status it left. With Plan 331 P1, the same open write atomically resets the spend window, optionally changes its ceiling in cents, and wakes the resident; close and place writes wake it too. A reason that would overflow the resulting note is refused whole, never truncated.
- **Place** — `PATCH /v1/spaces/:spaceId/plan/nodes/:nodeId/place`, `PlanNodePlaceSchema` `{ expectedRevision, before } | { expectedRevision, after }`, a sibling's id, puts the node directly before or after that sibling under the same parent (roots included). The engine (`placePlanNode`) reads the siblings in one transaction; a named node that is not a sibling any more is `409 PLAN_NODE_PLACE_STALE` with the siblings' order as it stands. Where no integer lies between the neighbours it renumbers the siblings at a stride of 1,024 in their existing order; that changes no sibling's place relative to another, so it writes no sibling's revision. The moved node's revision moves. **Move up** and **Move down** live where every sibling is on screen: on the plan board's Up next cards, whose read holds the whole plan, and on the plan list's open rows, which are every open root. Each sends the nearest open sibling above or below in position order, and shows only where one exists. The overview's Up next lane carries no reorder (D7). _Rejected:_ reorder on the overview's Up next rows — the lane holds one step per plan, so a step's siblings are not on screen and the write would name a node the operator cannot see. Audit action `plan.node.place`.

Both bump the attention generation and wake the Workbench topic as every plan write does. This builds Plan 331 D5's write, with one verb where 331 names two: **Approve** on every `blocked` node, because the record does not distinguish a proposal from a stop and the write and its effect are the same; Plan 331's document is edited to say so in the phase that lands it. Reparenting stays the Helmsman's (`plan.node.update`'s `parentId`). _Rejected:_ growing `PlanNodeCloseSchema` to accept `active` — the close write's rules (an outcome for done, a branch dropped) are a different act, and Plan 331 P1's criteria already refuse it. _Rejected:_ drag and drop as the only way to reorder — it cannot be done from a keyboard; a later drag sends the same write. _Rejected:_ the client sending absolute positions — two operators reordering would each overwrite the other's view of the order without either being refused.

**D7 — Two reads, sized to the view: the overview reads open steps and per-plan counts; the board reads one plan.** `GET /v1/spaces/:spaceId/plan` becomes the overview (`readPlanOverview`): every root as a row (`PlanRootRowSchema`: id, title, status, position, revision, `state`, counts `steps`, `needsYou`, `inFlight`, `upNext`, `done`, `dropped`, `lastWrittenAt`), and the three lanes as `PlanLaneItemSchema` rows (the node's id, title, kind, status, revision, `noteHead`, `updatedAt`, its root's id and title, its phase's title, the reason, the newest pull request, and for In flight its runs in flight with slug, status and start), Needs you and In flight each at most 50 with a total, Up next one row per open root — the step the walk would take, where it is idle and `active` — with no reorder control; ordering steps is the board's (D6). _Rejected:_ every idle `active` step in the Up next lane so that reorder works there — Plan 315 alone holds dozens, the lane would grow with the queue rather than with the number of plans, and what the loop takes next would no longer read at a glance. The store reads it in one recursive query from the roots that carries each node's root and whether a closed ancestor lies above it, returns open nodes as rows and closed ones only as counts per root, and joins in-flight run counts for the open nodes; done steps are never shipped. `GET /v1/spaces/:spaceId/plan/roots/:rootId` is the board (`readPlanBoard`): today's read scoped to one root, with `closedSince` and `tz` as now. Both keep `PLAN_TREE_WALK_BOUNDS`, and a bound reached is said on the page as now. `plan.node.*` reads are unchanged. _Rejected:_ the whole space's tree for every view — its cost grows with the space's history, not with what is on screen, and at fifty plans it truncates. _Rejected:_ a stored `root_id` column — every move would have to rewrite a subtree's rows, and a recursive query over at most 2,000 open nodes, counting the closed steps under them, does not need it.

**D8 — Where the operator is lives in the URL: `?plan=<rootId>&node=<nodeId>`.** No `plan` is the overview; `node` opens the rail over whichever view is showing. Reload, back and a link from anywhere — a Helmsman reply, a Workbench item, a help article — land on the same view. _Rejected:_ component state — what the page does now; nothing can point at a step. _Rejected:_ route segments (`/plan/[root]`) — both editions would need route files and manifest entries for one screen, and query parameters carry the same thing.

**D9 — The page is live: a plan write announces itself, and the page refreshes on it and on a run's change.** Every node write — create, update, link, open, place — publishes `entity.plan.updated` (`{ nodeId, revision }`) on the space's entity events after it commits, beside the Workbench wake. The page subscribes through `useSpaceEntityEvents` and invalidates `['space', spaceId, 'plan']` on it and on `entity.run.updated`, coalesced to one refetch a second. An open close or open form keeps the revision it showed, so a refresh never changes what a write is pinned to; a write against a node that moved is refused as now. _Rejected:_ polling — a fixed interval is either late or wasteful, and the events exist. _Rejected:_ re-reading only the node an event names — a node's column depends on its ancestors and descendants, and the overview's counts on the whole plan.

**D10 — Density: rows where the operator scans, compact cards where they read a plan, and one control per question.** The overview's lanes are one-line rows (plan code, title, reason or run, age, action). The board's cards keep their labels and lose nothing, at a minimum column width of 200px. The **Done** window goes: the folds (Today, Yesterday, earlier months) already answer "how far back", and two controls for one question meant a window could hide a fold the header counted. The **Phase** filter shows only where a plan has two or more phases. The four tally chips become the plan's header: its state, `closed of steps`, and its non-zero lane counts. _Rejected:_ keeping the window beside the folds — it filters what the folds already fold.

## 6. Phases

Each phase lands alone and in this order; P5 depends only on P1 and may land earlier.

**P0 — This document.** _Exit:_ merged, with its row in the index.

**P1 — The board's columns say what the loop does.** The column rule of D4 in `planBoardColumn` and in the tally (`needsYou`, `inFlight`, `upNext`, `nextNodeId`); `noteHead` on the tree node; stranded steps under Needs you with their reason; Up next in walk order with **Next** on the walk's step; columns ordered Needs you, In flight, Up next, Done, Dropped; the Activity filter, its two sentences and the Done window removed; the Phase filter only with two or more phases; the tally chips replaced by the header; the rail's **Now** and **Working note**; the help article. _Exit:_ on the live stack, Plan 315's idle `active` steps stand under Up next with the walk's step marked Next; a step with a commission running stands under In flight and returns to Up next when it ends (after a reload — P5 makes it live); Plan 320's four P6 steps read under Needs you as **Under a closed step**; Plan 331 P1's rail shows its "Proposed…" line under Now. _Criteria:_ the column rule tested on every row of D4's order, including `blocked` with a run in flight (Needs you), `waiting` with none (In flight), a `decide` node with a child running (Needs you) and an open step two levels under a closed one (stranded); the Next card equal to `findActiveStreamNode` over the same tree in a test that builds both; the page test with no Activity or Done window control.

**P2 — The operator approves, reopens and orders steps.** The open and place writes of D6, their schemas, engine function, routes and audit rows; **Approve** on a blocked step and **Reopen** on a closed one in the rail; **Reopen <parent>** on a stranded one; **Move up** / **Move down** on Up next cards among siblings; Plan 331's document updated. _Exit:_ on the live stack, the operator approves a blocked step from the rail and the next `plan.node.next` for its root reads it as the active node; moves one Up next step above its sibling and Next follows it; reopens a done step and finds its old outcome at the head of its note. _Criteria:_ route tests for each write — stamped from the session, an API key or a service principal `403 operator_only`, a stale revision `409` with `current`, an unchanged open `422`, a `before` that is no longer a sibling `409 PLAN_NODE_PLACE_STALE`, the audit row; engine tests for renumbering (order kept, only the moved node's revision written) and two concurrent places under one parent (one refused or both applied in an order neither loses).

**P3 — Plans are a list with a state.** `GET …/plan` returns the plan rows of D7 (lanes come in P4); the board reads `GET …/plan/roots/:rootId`; `state` derived per D3; the list of D2 with Finished and Closed sections, **Close plan** on finished rows (the existing close write on the root), **Move up** / **Move down** on open rows (P2's place write on roots); `?plan=` and `?node=` in the URL; the tab rail deleted. _Exit:_ on the live stack, every plan in the space is reachable at 1280px and at 900px; Plans 322, 324 and 328 (or whichever then have every step closed) list under Finished, and closing one moves it to Closed; reloading a URL with `plan` and `node` reopens the same board and rail; moving a plan up changes the order `plan.node.next` without a root reads (where Plan 331 has built it) and the list's after reload. _Criteria:_ the state rule tested for each state and for a plan both needing the operator and in flight; a board read for one root returns no other root's nodes; a list of 50 roots renders with the selected row in view.

**P4 — One view across plans.** `readPlanOverview`'s lanes; the overview as the landing view; lane rows with their inline actions and the shared rail. _Exit:_ on the live stack, the landing view lists every Needs-you step of every open plan with its reason and note line, and approving one from its row moves it to Up next or In flight without opening its plan; the In flight lane lists the running commission's step with its run. _Criteria:_ a test space of 50 roots with one blocked step in the last root shows it in the lane; a space with 5,000 done steps and 10 open returns no done rows and reaches no walk bound (Postgres suite); lane totals beyond 50 said on the page.

**P5 — The page is live.** `entity.plan.updated` on every node write; the page invalidates on it and on `entity.run.updated`, coalesced. _Exit:_ on the live stack, with the page open and no reload, a commission starting moves its step into In flight and its end moves it out, and a Helmsman write to a step's note changes its Now line. _Criteria:_ the event published once per create, update, link, open and place, after the commit, and not for a refused write; a form open in the rail keeps the revision it showed across a refresh.

## 7. How it is proven

- **The queue is the walk's.** A test builds a tree, runs `findActiveStreamNode` and the board's derivation, and fails if the card marked Next is any other node or if Up next's order differs from the walk's.
- **Up next is not empty while approved work waits.** The column rule test fails if an idle `active` step reads anywhere but Up next, or a `waiting` one anywhere but In flight.
- **Nothing waits on the operator unseen.** A blocked step in the fiftieth plan by position appears in the overview's Needs you lane; a step under a closed parent reads as stranded, not as an approvable block.
- **The operator unblocks without a conversation.** On the live stack, an approve from the page is the only write between a firing that waited on the blocked step and the next one that takes it.
- **Writes stay a person's and stay pinned.** An API key's open or place is refused whatever its grants; a write against a revision the Helmsman has moved past is refused and writes nothing.
- **The read scales with what is open.** The overview over a space with thousands of done steps returns their counts and none of their rows.
- **The page can be pointed at.** Reloading `?plan=…&node=…` restores the board and the rail.
